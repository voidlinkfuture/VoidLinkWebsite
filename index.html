// Mission

We started by launching rockets in the backyard and designing electronic components to understand physics through telemetry.

We get our hands dirty with science before we talk about it. The future is not something that happens: it is something you design, test and launch. In a few decades humanity will no longer be confined to a single planet, and intelligence, biological and artificial, will work at planetary scale and beyond.

We are not here to entertain. We are here to build that future.

// Edge Intelligence

One planet. One single point of failure.

Becoming multiplanetary is not a romantic dream: it is a backup of consciousness. Asteroids, supervolcanoes, pandemics, our own mistakes: Earth's history is full of events capable of wiping out a non-redundant civilization.

Field data collection is the first step toward edge-computing systems with autonomous AI for hostile environments, where latency to Earth makes remote control impossible. We train the operators.

We do not build rockets to compete: we build them to demonstrate, with data and not with promises, that physics is accessible, measurable and reproducible by anyone.
